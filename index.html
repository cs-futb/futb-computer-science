A department in context

Computing education, grounded in real problems.

The best technology begins with understanding the people and places it is meant to serve.

Get to know the department →

Based at the Federal University of Technology, Babura, in Jigawa State, the department sits within a university created to expand access to technology-focused higher education in north-western Nigeria.

Our academic community brings together teaching, software and systems thinking, data-driven approaches and emerging work in artificial intelligence. This website is designed to make that community easier to discover—for students, families, researchers and potential collaborators.

FROM THE DEPARTMENT ARCHIVETeaching practice matters.

In September 2024, the department hosted a seminar on the user-centred approach to the Core Curriculum and Minimum Academic Standards (CCMAS), led by Professor Mansur Aliyu.
